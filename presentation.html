Strategic Architectural Insight

Why Ambient Audio Scribing Cannot Solve Inpatient Care

Ambient scribing captures what is spoken during a 2-minute bedside visit. Inpatient care is an asynchronous multi-day saga.

🎙️
Tandem Ambient (Primary Care)
  • Synchronous capture: 1-on-1 dialogue during a single 10-minute outpatient appointment.
  • Single encounter: Patient and clinician talk, AI scribes live speech to clinical note.
  • Limitation in Acute Wards: Misses 90% of clinical events occurring away from the bedside.
🧠
Tandem Discharge (Secondary & Urgent Care)
  • Asynchronous synthesis: Integrates Day 1 admission, Day 2 labs, Day 3 microbiology, and Day 4 drug titrations.
  • Multi-disciplinary timeline: Consolidates nurse vitals, consultant plans, and pharmacy logs.
  • Deterministic reconciliation: Auditable diff of home vs. inpatient medication charts.
Together, Ambient + Discharge provide Tandem with a comprehensive Clinical OS covering 100% of the patient journey.

Clinical Breakthrough

Eliminating Medication Errors: The 4-State Med Rec Matrix

Medication errors at handover cost NHS lives and millions in readmissions. Tandem enforces a deterministic state machine for every drug.

STARTED
New Inpatient Therapy
E.g., Co-Amoxiclav 625mg TDS.

Requires mandatory indication and stop date / planned duration to prevent inappropriate antibiotic prolongation.
STOPPED
Discontinued Drug
E.g., Ramipril 5mg OD.

Requires explicit clinical rationale (e.g., AKI Stage 2) so receiving GPs do not inadvertently resume harmful medications.
DOSE CHANGED
Titrated Frequency / Dose
E.g., Furosemide doubled to 80mg OD.

Includes biochemical monitoring parameters and titration checkpoints for primary care follow-up.
CONTINUED
Maintained Home Meds
E.g., Atorvastatin 20mg ON.

Cross-referenced against admission clerking to ensure chronic disease medications are never omitted during stay.
Safety Standard: 0% tolerance for undocumented medication omissions Auditable by NHS Trust Pharmacy Lead
